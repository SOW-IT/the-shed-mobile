# Grievances

Anyone, signed in or not, can raise a grievance with SOW in confidence. This
context owns the grievance, how it moves along, and who is told. Like
[Design Requests](./design-requests.md) it owns no org structure: who handles
grievances comes from [Org](./org.md).

## Language

**Grievance**:
One complaint raised with SOW. Carries who raised it (name, email, optional
phone), what it's about (one of the form's options, kept in the words it was
raised with), what happened, and optional details: campus or area, when, who
was involved, and the outcome hoped for.
_Avoid_: complaint, ticket, report

**Reference**:
A random UUID each grievance gets. The team refers to a grievance by its
first eight characters; the submitter never sees it.
_Avoid_: number, ticket number

**Grievance team**:
Everyone in the current staff year's grievance departments, Heads included.
Admins choose the departments (People and Culture and Compliance to start);
the team alone sees every grievance, takes them on, gives them to each other,
closes them and edits the form's options. Admins outside those departments
can't see grievances.
_Avoid_: HR, handlers, reviewers

**Form options**:
The choices for "What is it about?", edited by the team. Changing them never
changes a grievance already raised.
_Avoid_: categories (in user-facing copy)

**Submitter**:
Whoever raised the grievance. Signed in, the grievance is filed under their
account and they follow it under Mine with push notifications; signed out,
they hear only by email. Either way they get an email at each stage.
_Avoid_: complainant, requester

**Stage**:
Submitted, then In progress (someone on the team has taken it on or been
given it), then Resolved (with an outcome note emailed to the submitter).
The team can instead close it as **Not proceeding** at any open stage, with a
reason. Passing a grievance to someone else on the team isn't a stage and
doesn't email the submitter.
_Avoid_: status (in user-facing copy), received, approved, declined

**Staff year** (of a grievance):
The year it's listed under on Submitted: the one it was raised in, until
1 October moves any still open into the new year (and the current year lists
them even before the move runs). The current staff year is always offered,
even with no grievances yet; earlier years only if grievances were listed in
them.
