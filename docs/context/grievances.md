# Grievances

Anyone, signed in or not, can raise a grievance with SOW in confidence. This
context owns the grievance, how it moves along, and who is told. Like
[Design Requests](./design-requests.md) it owns no org structure: who handles
grievances comes from [Org](./org.md).

## Language

**Grievance**:
One complaint raised with SOW, numbered across all years ("Grievance #4").
Carries who raised it (name, email, optional phone), what it's about (a
category), what happened, and optional details: campus or area, when, who was
involved, and the outcome hoped for.
_Avoid_: complaint, ticket, report

**Grievance team**:
Everyone in the People and Culture or Compliance departments for the current
staff year, Heads included. They alone see every grievance, take them on, give
them to each other and close them. Admins outside those departments can't see
grievances.
_Avoid_: HR, handlers, reviewers

**Submitter**:
Whoever raised the grievance. Signed in, the grievance is filed under their
account and they follow it on the Grievances page; signed out, they hear only
by email. Either way they get an email at every stage.
_Avoid_: complainant, requester

**Stage**:
Submitted, then Received (someone in the grievance team has taken it on or
been given it), In progress, then Resolved (with an outcome note emailed to
the submitter). The team can instead close it as **Not proceeding** at any
open stage, with a reason. Passing a grievance to someone else on the team
isn't a stage and doesn't email the submitter.
_Avoid_: status (in user-facing copy), approved, declined

**Grievances inbox**:
grievances@sow.org.au, a Google Group of the grievance team. It gets every
new grievance in full and a line at every stage; the submitter's emails reply
to it.
