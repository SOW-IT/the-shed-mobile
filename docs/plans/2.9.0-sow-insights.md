# 2.9.0 plan: SOW Insights for TWIGs and PPNs

Status: agreed with the product owner on 2026-10-15, not started. This is the
brief for whoever builds it. Read [weekly-insights.md](../weekly-insights.md)
and [attendance-metrics.md](../attendance-metrics.md) first; most of the
pieces below already exist there.

## What we're building

Insights → Attendance with **SOW** selected gets a switcher at the top:
**Campuses | TWIG | PPN**.

- **Campuses** is today's SOW view, unchanged. The range picker and the
  collaborative switch (`AttendanceRangeFab`) show on this view only.
- **TWIG**: SOW's TWIGs for one staff year, counted on campus leaders.
- **PPN**: SOW's monthly PPNs for one staff year, counted on everyone.

Words used below:

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| TWIG event | An event with the Other-events **TWIG** tag and **without** the PPN tag. |
| PPN event | An event with **both** the TWIG and the Other-events **PPN** tags (a "TWIG PPN"). A campus event tagged PPN alone, like Intercampus PPN, is not one. |
| Held | At least one sign-in. An event with 0 sign-ins is left out of every number and listed under "No roll taken". |
| Staff year | 1 Oct to 30 Sep (`staffYearForDate`, [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-october-1-staff-year-rollover.md)). Compared with the staff year before. |
| Campus leader | Has a staff profile for that staff year with a university-scoped role: `assignmentsOf(profile).some((a) => roleNeedsUniversity(a.role))`, the same check as `isCampusLeader` in `convex/directory.ts`. |
| Organiser | Has a staff profile that staff year but no university-scoped role. |
| No staff profile | No staff profile for that staff year (or a sign-in with no member). |
| Campus | A leader's first university-scoped assignment that staff year. They count once, under that campus. |

The 2.8.0 migration already tagged every SOW monthly PPN and both Farewell
Events with TWIG + PPN, and Intercampus PPN with PPN only, so prod data is
ready.

## TWIG view

Only **campus leaders** count in the numbers. Organisers are shown but not
counted and not flagged. People with no staff profile are flagged.

1. **Cards** (with change vs the staff year before, as a pill):
   - **Average attendance**: campus-leader sign-ins per held TWIG.
   - **People**: distinct campus leaders who came to at least one.
   - **Regulars**: campus leaders at half or more of the year's held TWIGs
     (`regularShare`, 0.5).
   - No first-timers card.
2. **TWIG by TWIG**: one bar per held TWIG (campus leaders only), with the
   previous staff year's TWIGs in grey in the same position (1st with 1st,
   2nd with 2nd). Labelled by date.
3. **By campus**: campus-leader sign-ins split by campus (`breakdown`).
4. **Needs follow-up**: regulars who missed the last 2 held TWIGs
   (`followUpMisses`, 2).
5. **Everyone**: searchable, grouped Campus leaders / Organisers / No staff
   profile. Each row: "6/8 · 75%" of held TWIGs and when they last came.
   No-staff-profile rows carry the warning "No staff profile for 2026".
6. **No roll taken**: TWIGs that happened with 0 sign-ins, by date.

## PPN view

**Everyone** counts. Non-leaders are noted, never flagged.

1. **One row per held PPN, newest first**: date and name; sign-ins (everyone);
   change vs the same calendar month in the staff year before ("+12 vs Mar
   2025", or nothing when that month had no PPN); first-timers (their first
   PPN ever, across all years); split by campus; split by Campus leaders /
   Staff / Others.
2. A caption with the year's totals, e.g. "36 sign-ins from people with no
   staff profile" (a note, not a warning).
3. **No roll taken**, as in TWIG.

Campus for an "other" (no staff profile) is their member Campus field
(`CAMPUS_FIELD_KEY`), else "No campus".

## What prod looks like (2026-10-15)

| | Staff year 2025 | Staff year 2026 | Staff year 2027 so far |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TWIGs held / 0 sign-ins | 6 / 1 | 8 / 5 | 1 / 0 |
| TWIG sign-ins: campus leaders / organisers / no profile | 111 / 14 / 1 | 223 / 46 / 2 | 31 / 6 / 0 |
| PPNs held | 8 | 10 | 0 |
| PPN sign-ins: campus leaders / staff / no profile | 182 / 174 / 21 | 228 / 221 / 36 | |

No 2026 leader has university roles at two campuses. In total there are about
40 TWIG/PPN events and 1,500 sign-ins; the whole dataset is around 276 events
and 13,000 sign-ins. That size is why the plan below computes live.

## Design

### Backend: one live query

`convex/sowInsights.ts`:

```ts
export const view = query({
  args: { mode: v.union(v.literal("twig"), v.literal("ppn")), staffYear: v.optional(v.number()) },
  returns: v.union(v.null(), v.object({ blocks: v.array(weeklyBlockValidator), years: v.array(v.number()) })),
  handler: ...
});
```

- Same access as `attendanceMetrics.view`: `optionalProfile`, and SOW
  Insights is staff-only already.
- Reads events by `by_dateStart` across the two staff years
  (`staffYearStartMs(year - 1)` to `staffYearStartMs(year + 1)`), keeps the
  TWIG/PPN ones by tag name (look the two tags up by `name` and
  `list: "events"`, never by id). For PPN first-timers it also needs every
  earlier PPN: read events before the window once, it's small.
- Sign-ins: `attendance.by_event` for each kept event.
- People: reuse `resolvePeople` from `convex/weeklyInsights.ts` (export it, or
  move it to a shared server module). It already reads staff profiles by year,
  skips unassigned ones (`isAssigned`), picks the first university-scoped
  assignment as the campus, and falls back to the member's Campus field.
  Call it with staff years.
- No snapshot table, no cron. It's reactive, so a sign-in shows up straight
  away. If it ever gets slow, copy the `weeklyInsightViews` pattern
  (precompute nightly, read one row), but at today's size that's not needed.
- `years`: the staff years that have any TWIG or PPN, newest first, for the
  year picker.

### Pure logic and blocks

- `shared/sowInsights.ts`: classify each sign-in (campus leader / organiser /
  no profile), held vs no-roll events, TWIG cards, regulars, follow-up,
  per-PPN rows, first-timers, the same-month comparison. Pure functions, unit
  tested.
- `shared/sowInsightsView.ts`: turns that into blocks, reusing what's there:
  `cards`, `multiBars` (this year vs last), `breakdown`, `followUpGroups`,
  `people` (sections, filters, search already work), `eventList` (No roll
  taken), `caption`.
- One new block type for the PPN rows, e.g. `{ type: "monthRows"; title;
  rows: { title; subtitle; value; delta?; chips: { label; value }[] }[] }`,
  drawn in `WeeklyBlocks.tsx` or `InsightsBlocks.tsx`. Older apps skip
  unknown block types, which is fine because they never ask for this view.

### App

- `src/components/attendance/MetricsTab.tsx`: when `orgWide`, show a
  `Segmented` (Campuses / TWIG / PPN) above the blocks. Campuses keeps the
  current `attendanceMetrics.view` query; TWIG and PPN call
  `sowInsights.view`.
- `src/app/(tabs)/insights.tsx`: keep the SOW mode in state (lift it up so the
  floating buttons know it). On Campuses show `AttendanceRangeFab` as today;
  on TWIG/PPN show a staff-year picker instead (`PeriodFab` with one option
  per staff year works, labelled "2026 (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026)").
- Tapping a person opens `/attendance/member/[key]` as on campus views.
- Remember the last mode per device if it's cheap (try/catch, as elsewhere).

## Edge cases

- A staff year with no held TWIG (2027 on 1 Oct): an `empty` block,
  "No TWIGs yet this staff year", plus No roll taken if any.
- Comparison while the year is running: cards compare with the previous
  staff year **up to the same date** (as weekly Insights does with "up to
  T3 W4"), and say so in the card's (i). The TWIG-by-TWIG chart pairs by
  position, so it doesn't need that.
- A sign-in with no member (`memberId` missing) counts as No staff profile
  and as its own anonymous person in PPN totals.
- An event tagged TWIG on a campus-owned event: still a TWIG (tag is the
  rule). Today every TWIG/PPN is SOW-owned.
- The rollover: the query takes the staff year from `currentStaffYear()` by
  default, so it flips with the clock and needs nothing on 1 Oct.

## Tests

- `shared/sowInsights.test.ts`: every rule above, including a person who is
  a campus leader one year and an organiser the next, the no-member sign-in,
  the PPN with no same-month last year, and a TWIG with 0 sign-ins.
- `convex/sowInsights.test.ts` (convex-test): tag lookup by name and list
  (PPN exists in both lists), staff-only access, the two-year window, empty

  branches 92).

  check the numbers match the table above, and check the range picker only
  shows on Campuses.
